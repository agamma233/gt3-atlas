## 3. Data sources / 数据来源

Technical specifications, race results, entry lists and series standings are compiled from the sources linked in the site. The project clearly marks:

- manufacturer base values versus race-specific values;
- power figures with their original units and BoP context;
- missing or unpublished fields;
- version and livery-year differences;
- the scope of each comparison result.

While the archive aims for accuracy, no warranty is made that every field is complete, current, or free of error. Official series bulletins, BoP documents and manufacturer technical releases take precedence.
